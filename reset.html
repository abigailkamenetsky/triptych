Clearing the old copy

Triptych stores itself on your device so it works with no internet. That stored copy can get stuck on an old version. This clears it and fetches the newest one. Nothing in your library is touched.

Emptying the library

The step above never touches your books, because a reader who has lost a library to a refresh does not get it back. This does: it removes every book, bookmark and saved page from this device, so the app looks exactly as it will on a device that has never seen it. There is no undo.
