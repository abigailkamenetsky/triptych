# Your border art goes here

Drop illuminated border images into this folder and run:

    python3 art/frames.py

Anything readable works: PNG, JPEG or WebP, portrait or landscape, with a
clear centre for the text. Landscape plates are used on iPad held sideways,
portrait on a phone or an iPad held upright.

Anything in here takes precedence over the plates in `art/generated/`, which
`art/plates.py` composes from the cut Bosch figures. Nothing is overwritten,
so you can put art here and take it away again freely.
